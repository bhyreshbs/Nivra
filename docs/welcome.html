Nivra is installed

Thanks for trying it. Here is how to get the most out of it.

1. Open any PDF

With Nivra turned on, PDF links and web addresses ending in .pdf open in Nivra automatically. Use the display modes (Smart Dark, Invert, Grayscale, High Contrast) from the bar at the bottom of the viewer. Your original file is never changed.

2. Turn on local PDFs

To open PDFs stored on your computer, switch on Allow access to file URLs for Nivra. The browser does not let extensions do this for you.

  1. Open edge://extensions
    (Browsers block links to this page, so paste it into the address bar.)
  2. Find Nivra and click Details.
  3. Turn on Allow access to file URLs.

You can also open a local PDF without this step: click the Nivra toolbar icon and drop the file on the popup.

3. Pin the icon

Click the puzzle-piece (Extensions) button in the toolbar and pin Nivra. The popup has the on/off switch: when it is off, PDFs open in Edge'sChrome's own viewer again.

Private by design

PDFs are processed on your device and never uploaded. No accounts, no tracking. Read the privacy policy.

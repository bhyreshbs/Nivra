Nivra — the mark

The vermilion disc is the sun or the moon, printed a hair off-register like a two-colour woodblock print. Take your screenshot of the large one (or use the PNG buttons below).

What is a "promotional tile"?

In the Edge Add-ons store, a promotional tile is an optional banner image (small: 440×280, large: 1400×560). Microsoft uses it on the store home page, in collections and in search results when it features an extension — it is the "poster" for your listing. You do not need one to publish, but listings with one look more finished, and it is the only way to get featured.
